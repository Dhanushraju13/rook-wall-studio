Our Story

WHO WE ARE

Rook Wall Studio is a creative production house focused on developing, producing and delivering original stories for audiences across screens.

Founded on the belief that every story deserves to be told with honesty and craft, we bring together talented writers, directors, cinematographers and crew to build productions that resonate.

Studio FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers about working with Rook Wall Studio.

What types of productions does Rook Wall Studio undertake?

We produce feature films, short films, documentaries, and branded content. Our slate spans dramatic narratives, genre cinema, and experimental visual work that pushes the boundaries of storytelling.

How can I submit a script or project proposal?

Send your one-page synopsis, logline, and contact details via our Contact Studio page. We review all submissions within 4–6 weeks and respond to projects that align with our creative vision.

Are there internship or crew opportunities available?

Yes. We regularly welcome emerging filmmakers, editors, sound designers, and production assistants. Visit our Careers page for open positions and internship programmes.

Does the studio offer commercial or branded content services?

Absolutely. Our services team creates high-impact branded films, corporate documentaries, and advertising campaigns. Explore our full capabilities on the Services page.
